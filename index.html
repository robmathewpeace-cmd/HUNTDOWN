A club built around reading, not promotion.

This section explains what HUNT DOWN WE READ is, where it lives, and what we believe a book club should do.

Why we live on StoryGraph

HUNT DOWN WE READ is intentionally centered within StoryGraph because our primary purpose is reading and reader engagement rather than maintaining a large promotional presence across multiple platforms.

What this means for you: one home for meetings, forums, and the leaderboard, with nothing scattered across apps.

What we believe

We are a reader-focused community built around one fundamental belief: we don't just read books. We hunt for meaning, questions, growth, and transformation within them. A book is not simply something to finish. Every story can become a journey. Every page can become a clue. Every argument can become a question. Every reader can bring a different perspective to the same work.

What this means for you: you are invited to read slowly, notice details, and trust your own interpretation.

How our discussions work

Our discussions are designed to go beyond whether someone liked or disliked a book. We encourage readers to examine the author's ideas, question assumptions, identify themes, discuss important passages, connect the work to real-life issues, and consider how the book changes or challenges their perspective.

What this means for you: every thread gives you real questions to answer, not just a rating to give.

Why most book clubs fade, and what we do differently.

Many clubs start with energy and quietly stop meeting. This section shows the problem and how we are built to avoid it.

You found the book, made the time, and showed up ready for a real conversation. Then came the small talk: "Did you like it?" "It was good." Half the group had not finished, the loudest voice set the direction, and by the third month the club had stopped meeting.

The problem is rarely the readers. It is that most clubs have no structure, no real questions, and no reason to keep showing up.

Ideas that change us usually need another mind to push against: a different interpretation, a respectful disagreement, a question we would never have asked ourselves.

Good conversations are built, not hoped for. We give ours clear rules, thoughtful prompts, a steady rhythm, and a community that values depth over speed.

How you join the hunt, step by step.

Five steps take you from joining to being recognized. Each step below says when it happens and what you do.

1
To get started

Join us on StoryGraph

Create a StoryGraph account, open the HUNT DOWN WE READ club page, and join. Read the Code of Conduct first so you know the standard we hold together: respect every perspective, think deeply, and speak thoughtfully.

2
Each month

Pick up the featured read

Every month we feature curated reads across genres, from classics like Of Mice and Men to modern voices and independent authors. You read at your own pace, because this is about depth, consistency, and perspective, not speed.

3
Weekly

Join the deep-dive discussion

Each featured book has its own forum with written prompts about character, theme, ethics, and meaning. Disagree with ideas, never with people, and add a spoiler warning before discussing major plot points.

4
Every quarter

Take on the reading challenge

Our Quarterly Reading Challenge stretches your reading. Read across multiple genres, include at least one classic and one modern voice, take part in weekly discussions, and share at least one personal reflection or takeaway.

5
End of each season

Vote, reflect, and be recognized

Readers vote in the Community Choice Book Awards, and standout readers and reviews are celebrated in the StoryGraph Reader Spotlight. Your voice shapes which books the club remembers.

Why readers choose HUNT DOWN WE READ.

Five reasons, each tied to something the club does every week, including how we look after the authors who join us.

Open to debut novelists, independent authors, hybrid authors, and small presses across genres.

🔍

We read for meaning, not just completion.

Finishing a book is an achievement. Understanding it is a different one. Our prompts take you below the plot and into the ideas that make a story last.

🤝

Every perspective is welcome and respected.

New readers and lifelong readers hold equal standing. We challenge ideas, not people, and a different interpretation is often another layer of the story.

🌍

Genres, eras, and voices from everywhere.

One season may bring Steinbeck, the next Kuang, then a memoir or a debut thriller. The variety is deliberate and keeps your reading curious.

🔥

Your voice actually matters here.

Readers vote on the awards, shape the discussions, and can be featured in the Reader Spotlight. Books here are more than entertainment.

🛡️

We treat your participation fee as if it were our own money.

An author's trust is not something we take lightly. Every participation fee goes toward running the challenge: curating your book's reading track, writing discussion prompts, moderating conversations, and administering the voting and awards. This is not a profit-making exercise.

You will know exactly what the fee covers before you pay, you are welcome to ask us any question first, and there is no pressure to commit after your first message. Your book is handled with the same care we would want for our own work.

What you will receive as a participating author

This is not a passive "feature." Your book becomes part of a living, breathing reading experience, and the benefits are tangible.

Verified reader engagement

Twenty to fifty or more of our verified, committed members will purchase and read your book, bringing genuine support and real sales.

Thoughtful reviews and feedback

You will receive detailed, authentic reflections, not one-line ratings. Our discussion prompts guide readers to put into words what moved them, what challenged them, and how the book shaped their perspective. Readers are honest, so positive reviews are never promised.

Spotlight opportunities

Your book and your story as an author are highlighted in our StoryGraph Reader Spotlight and community newsletters, putting your work in front of an audience that values substance over hype.

Award potential

Your book becomes eligible for our Community Choice Book Award, a distinction that carries weight within our community and beyond as we grow our public presence. Readers vote, so eligibility is never a promise of winning.

Social and media exposure

Highlights, quotes, and reflections from the challenge are shared across our StoryGraph page and social channels, amplifying your book's reach to intentional readers.

A lasting literary footprint

Long after the quarter ends, your book remains part of our discussion archives, where new members continue to discover and engage with past featured titles.

Beyond numbers and visibility, you gain something rarer: a group of readers who will genuinely wrestle with your book's message and carry it into their own lives.

Participation fee and exactly what it covers

To maintain the integrity and depth of this experience, there is a one-time participation fee. This is not a profit-making exercise. The fee directly covers:

  • Curation and dedicated management of your book's reading track for the full quarter
  • Creation of custom discussion prompts and thematic guides
  • Moderation of all discussions and community engagement
  • Your book's place in the StoryGraph Reader Spotlight and award voting alongside every other challenge book, where readers decide the outcome
  • Promotional features across our community platforms
  • Administrative costs associated with running the challenge, voting, and awards

Everything we do is designed to honor your work and provide a reading experience that goes far beyond what a typical book club offers. The fee ensures that your book receives the focus, respect, and visibility it deserves without relying on ads or algorithms.

To be clear: the payment is only for joining our Quarterly Reading Challenge membership and the running of the challenge. It does not buy a review, a rating, an award, or any particular result, and featured books still go through our review process.
